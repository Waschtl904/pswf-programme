\section{What this paper proves}

The central claim is a \emph{spectral structure theorem}, not an error estimate.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Old framing (superseded):}
$G_N \approx I$ (quadrature accuracy statement).

\noindent\textbf{New framing:}
$G_N^{\mathrm{edge}} = I + \lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}(c)\,P_{e_c} + R_c^\perp$
(rank-one spectral resonance).

The full sampling Gram matrix has block spectral structure
\[
  \sigma(G_N)
  = \bigl\{1 + \lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}(c)\bigr\}
  \cup
  [1-\delta,\,1+\delta]^{N-1},
  \quad \delta = O(c^{-1/2}).
\]
