\begin{abstract}
We study the discrete sampling Gram matrix
$G_N[j,k] = c^{-1}\sum_{n=-c}^{c}
\varphi_j(n/c;c)\,\varphi_k(n/c;c)$,
where $\{\varphi_k(\,\cdot\,;c)\}$ are the $L^2([-1,1])$-normalised
prolate spheroidal wave functions with bandwidth $c$.
The classical picture $G_N \approx I$ holds in the bulk sector
but fails for above-barrier modes ($\chi_k > c^2$):
rather than producing a small perturbation of the identity,
the edge sector admits the rank-one decomposition
\[
  G_N^{\mathrm{edge}} = I + \lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}(c)\,P_{e_c} + R_c^\perp,
\]
where $e_c = (\varphi_k(1;c))_{k\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{edge}}}$
is the boundary profile vector,
$\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}(c)$ is numerically dominant and growing,
and $\|R_c^\perp\|_{\mathrm{op}}/\lambda_{\mathrm{eff}}(c) \le 0.010$.
By the Davis--Kahan theorem, the unique unstable eigenvector
converges to the boundary profile direction:
$|\langle v_1,\, e_c/\|e_c\|\rangle| \ge 0.9975$.
This is a \emph{boundary spectral decomposition} of a singularly
discretized band-limiting operator, not a quadrature error result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

\subsection{The mechanism in one chain}
\[
\chi_k > c^2
\Rightarrow \varphi_k(1;c)\nearrow\infty
\Rightarrow \tfrac{2}{c}\varphi_j(1)\varphi_k(1) \text{ dominates}
\Rightarrow \lambda_1(G_N^{\mathrm{edge}})\nearrow\infty.
\]

\subsection{What this explains}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Exactly one eigenvalue diverges (rank-one $F_c$).
\item Bulk modes remain stable ($\varphi_k(1;c)$ bounded below barrier).
\item Instability begins above the barrier ($\chi_k > c^2$).
\item Unstable direction = boundary profile (Davis--Kahan).
\item $G_N \approx I$ fails in the edge sector, holds in the bulk.
\end{enumerate}
